Find every mole

A cozy logic puzzle about moles. Place one mole in every row, column and garden bed, and keep them apart. No guessing and no maths: every level has one solution you reach by pure logic.

Coming soon to the App Store
iPhone · iPad · Free with optional in-app purchases

The rules

One mole in every row and every column.
One mole in every colored garden bed.
Moles never touch, not even diagonally.

What’s inside

5000 levels

200 chapters from a cozy 5×5 to a tricky 10×10, each with its own scenery.

Daily challenge

A new board every day with a twist for each weekday, and a streak to keep.

Hints that teach

Where to look, then why, and only then the move. New techniques come with a short lesson.

League and events

A weekly league, weekly events, sprint, marathon and chapter trials.

Collect

12 costumes, 12 rare moles in the Molepedia, golden moles and 50 achievements.

Plays your way

Expert mode, bold bed patterns for color-blind players, light and dark themes.

Widgets and Siri

Today’s challenge and your streak on the Home Screen.

No sign-in

Puzzles work offline. Progress syncs between your devices through iCloud.

По-русски

Кротоку — уютная логическая головоломка про кротов. Поставьте ровно одного крота в каждую строку, каждый столбец и каждую цветную грядку так, чтобы кроты не касались друг друга даже по диагонали. Ни угадывания, ни арифметики: у каждого уровня одно решение. 5000 уровней в 200 главах, вызов дня, недельная лига, костюмы, кротопедия и 50 достижений. Без регистрации, играть можно офлайн, прогресс синхронизируется через iCloud.
